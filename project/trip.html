Trip Planning and Safety

What are some to-dos before you head out on your trip?

Pre trip planning | What to pack | On the water safety

Pre trip planning

Before heading out, check the weather and marine forecast, and keep watching the sky for developing storms once you're on the water. If you're boating, file a float plan with someone on land detailing where you're going, when you'll return, and who's aboard. Confirm your license is current and check the regulations for the specific water you're fishing, since rules can vary by location. Finally, build a trip-specific gear list matched to your target species and location rather than bringing everything you own.

What to pack

Personal flotation devices are essential for everyone aboard, often legally required and always safety-critical. Bring a first aid kit stocked with bandages, antiseptic, tweezers, gloves, and pain relief, along with sun protection like polarized sunglasses, sunscreen, and a wide-brim hat. Pack weather-appropriate layers, non-slip footwear, and a dry bag for spare clothes, plus a fully charged phone in a waterproof case and a signalling device like a whistle. If you're planning to keep your catch, don't forget a cooler, fillet knife, and bags.

On the water safety

Wear your life jacket for the entire trip, not just when conditions turn rough, and distribute weight evenly in small boats to avoid overloading. If you're operating a boat, know the basic navigation rules, and consider getting a free vessel safety check from the Coast Guard before a big trip. Where possible, avoid fishing alone, and supervise kids closely with gear suited to their age.
